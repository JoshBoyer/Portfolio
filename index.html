About

I’m a systems analyst and developer based near Ottawa, Canada, with a background in insurance technology — requirements, business analysis, and building the software that turns weeks-long processes into minutes. I hold a Business Analysis & IT Project Management certificate from McMaster and I’m a certified Professional Scrum Master.

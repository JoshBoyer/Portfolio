About

I’ve spent 18 years in insurance technology across P&C and health, specialising in integration design and core-system modernisation. Today I work in claims architecture at Medavie Blue Cross, where I’ve owned integration, migration, and configuration workstreams on an enterprise claims platform modernisation that brought 35M+ claims and a million members onto a modern core. Before that I architected a P&C carrier’s complete rating and underwriting rule set and its broker integrations. I’m a certified Professional Scrum Master with a certificate in business analysis.

Outside the day job I build things I actually use: privacy-first apps that run on my own hardware, on-device AI tools, and physical projects from the Workshop 42 bench. A recurring theme: local-first, self-hosted, and no data leaving the house without a good reason.
